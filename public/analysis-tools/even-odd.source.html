Entry and exit

No resolved signals yet. They will appear here once a signal crosses the threshold and the next tick arrives.

Each time a signal at or above the threshold appears, it is compared with the parity of the next real tick. With few signals the result fluctuates a lot; wait for at least 100 before drawing conclusions.

How confidence is calculated
  • Count difference (40 pts): how strongly one parity dominates the window. Maximum at 65% or more.
  • Dominant digit (25 pts): the most frequent digit has that parity and beats the expected 10%. Maximum at 20%.
  • Top 3 digits (20 pts): how many of the three most frequent digits have that parity.
  • Last 20 ticks (15 pts): the parity holds in the most recent ticks. Maximum at 75%.
  • Confidence = 50% + 49% × (points / 100). High evidence from 80%, medium from 65%.
  • Deriv synthetic ticks are random and independent. This tool describes what happened in the window; it does not guarantee what will happen next. Check the backtest before risking money.
