Entry and exit

No resolved signals yet. They will appear here once a signal crosses the threshold and the next tick arrives.

When a signal locks, the tool waits for its entry digit to show as the last digit, records the trade on that tick, and checks the next real tick: Over wins if the digit is above the barrier, Under wins if it is below. Compare the win rate with the fair rate of each signal. Wait for at least 100 signals before drawing conclusions.

How the signal is calculated
  • Everything comes from the market you picked: the last 1,000 ticks of that market (set the window above). Switching market reloads its own history.
  • Only easier barriers are tested: Over 0–3 and Under 6–9, where the fair win chance is 60% or more. Hard ones like Over 7 or Under 2 are never shown. Over d wins on digits above d; Under d wins on digits below d.
  • Ranking: by z-score, how many standard errors this market's win rate is above the fair rate. This compares Over 2 and Over 7 fairly.
  • Entry digit: for the chosen barrier, the tool looks at the tick right after every last digit 0–9 in the window and picks the digit with the best conservative win rate (lower end of the interval), so a digit with few samples cannot win by luck. It does not switch around: the digit only changes if another becomes clearly better, and a signal keeps its entry digit until the signal ends.
  • A signal is active only when all 6 checks pass: win rate beats fair by the minimum edge; the adjusted chance of the result is 1 in 20 or rarer (adjusted for testing all barriers); the last 100 ticks win at or above fair; every losing digit is at or below 10.5% (for Under 7 that is 7, 8 and 9; for Over 2 it is 0, 1 and 2); the entry digit has at least 30 samples; the entry digit wins more often than a random tick.
  • Signals stay put: once a signal shows, it stays (and trades repeat on its entry digit) until it is no longer valid, meaning the market's win rate falls to fair, the chance of it being luck rises above 1 in 10, or a losing digit climbs above 11.5%. Only then can a new signal appear.
  • The big percentage is the real win rate this market produced in the window, not a score. The badge shows how rare that result is by luck.
  • Deriv synthetic ticks are generated randomly and independently, so a digit that did well in the past window may not keep doing well. Check the backtest and the "by chance" figure before risking money.
